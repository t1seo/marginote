## Long description

Keep explanations and linked notes beside the passage you are reading. Marginote connects a word or phrase to a floating Markdown card with a fine outline and line, so its source stays clear.

Create a text card, add a local image, or combine both. You can also preview existing note links with a title, path, short excerpt, and an action to open the full note. Choose annotation cards only, ordinary notes only, or both. Proximity previews follow the pointer through nearby text in Reading view and Live Preview; click the link or use the keyboard to keep a card open. Prefer a stationary preview? Choose the option that opens only when you hover directly over a link, or turn automatic previews off.

Cards are ordinary Markdown files linked from your note. Unlinking a card restores its visible phrase without deleting the card or its images. Marginote works locally without an account, network service, telemetry, or payment. Reading view and Live Preview are supported on desktop Obsidian 1.10.6 or later; mobile is not supported in this release.
